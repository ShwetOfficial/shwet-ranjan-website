4. KEY FINANCIAL & BUSINESS FORMULAS
================================================================================
- GST Cash Lock: Output Tax = (Gross Sales * Tax Rate) / 100
  Raw ITC = (Purchases * Tax Rate) / 100
  Locked ITC = Raw ITC * (Delinquent Vendor % / 100)
  Eligible ITC = Raw ITC - Locked ITC
  Net Cash Tax Payable = max(0, Output Tax - Eligible ITC)

- D2C CM3 Margin: COD % = 100 - Prepaid %
  RTO Freight Cost = Forward Freight * 1.6
  Weighted Freight = (Forward Freight * (1 - RTO %)) + (RTO Freight * RTO %)
  Gateway Fee = Selling Price * 2%
  CM3 Margin = Selling Price - COGS - Weighted Freight - Gateway Fee - CAC
  CM3 Margin % = (CM3 Margin / Selling Price) * 100
